## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, distinctive master logo for Desato, a Brazilian app helping people take steps toward stopping drinking. Asset: standalone primary brand logo, real usable flat artwork on transparent background, not a brand presentation or mockup. Design a custom bold geometric D monogram that suggests an open cycle and a clear path forward: a strong rounded D silhouette with one precise diagonal opening near the upper right and carefully designed negative space; restrained, confident, memorable, visually balanced and legible at small app-icon sizes. Beside the symbol, the exact word 'Desato', with exceptional custom contemporary humanist sans-serif typography, softly rounded yet strong, excellent kerning, lowercase after capital D. Existing brand color direction: deep evergreen #173E32 with a restrained fresh sage #80A889 accent only if useful. Premium clarity and maturity, no clinical or wellness cliches. Horizontal balanced lockup, symbol and wordmark comfortably separated, generous transparent margins, large clean crisp shapes. One final logo only. No leaf, heart, medical cross, drink/glass, chains, generic swoosh, literal arrow icon, gradients, shadows, 3D, lighting effects, paper textures, extra labels, slogan, watermark or checkerboard baked into image. Text must read exactly Desato.
